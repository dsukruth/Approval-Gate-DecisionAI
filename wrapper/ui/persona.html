    The gate returns APPROVE, HOLD FOR REVIEW or BLOCK with a checklist of every signal, each sentence with its findings, and a suggested fix when a price or policy value is wrong. The mock email is sent only on APPROVE.

    What the gate checks

    Prices and policies

    Prices, discounts and eligibility, stacking, refunds, free trials and first response times are compared with price_sheet.csv and policies.json. A contradiction blocks.

    • $19 / $49 / $99 per month for Starter, Pro, Business
    • Loyalty 15% for Pro or Business with 12+ months
    • Annual billing 20%, discounts never stack
    • Refunds 30 days monthly, 14 days annual
    • Free trial 14 days on Pro and Business
    • First response 48h / 24h / 4h

    Promises, hype and flattery

    Promising features, delivery dates or exceptions holds the email for a manager.

    Hype, guarantees and agreeing with a customer's wrong claim also hold it, even when every number is right.

    Ezio public fact-checks

    Ezio checks public claims such as laws and regulations. A contradicted claim blocks. A supported claim needs nothing more.

    Ezio tone, bias and agreement

    A score below the minimums in policy.json holds the email for review.

    Ezio overall tier

    Ezio's tier maps to a decision in policy.json. Across all signals the strictest one wins: BLOCK, then HOLD FOR REVIEW, then APPROVE.

    Laya (optional)

    A local model that can only add a hold when it reads a promise. It never approves or blocks, and shows as skipped when it is off.

    When Ezio is down the gate fails closed: it never approves, it holds for review, or blocks when our own data already proves a fact wrong. This page never decides. Every decision comes from the wrapper's POST /decide, and the mock send_email action runs on the server only after APPROVE.
